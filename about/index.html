About CleanQR

A utility platform built on the belief that everyday digital tools should be fast, honest, and completely private.

Why We Built CleanQR

If you have ever searched for a "free QR code generator" on Google, you have likely encountered the bait-and-switch trap. You spend time creating a code for a menu, flyer, or business card, only to discover weeks later that the code has stopped working. The service demands a $20 to $50 monthly subscription to keep your own links functional.

CleanQR was built to eliminate this predatory model. By generating pure static QR codes directly in your browser using standard open-source encoding specifications, your destination content is burned directly into the graphic. There is no intermediate server, no redirect link, and no monthly renewal fee.

Core Principles

100% In-Browser Execution

Your URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, contact details, and images never touch our servers. Everything is rendered client-side with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript.

No Account Required

You never need to create an account, verify an email, or provide personal payment information to download high-resolution assets.

Permanent Validity

Because static codes do not rely on a hosted redirect service, they will continue working for the lifetime of your printed materials.

Zero Watermarks

Every exported PNG, JPEG, and SVG is pristine and completely unbranded. You have full commercial ownership of your graphics.
